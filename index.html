How to use the typing animation generator

Typing Animation is a typing animation generator for video intros, social posts, presentations and animated text on the web. Start with your own message or one of 24 editable templates. Edit and preview without an account; sign in to download.

  1. Write your message

    Enter up to 1,000 characters in the Text tab. Use line breaks and emoji, or record your own typing rhythm to capture natural pauses.

  2. Shape the typewriter effect

    Choose from 32 open-source fonts. Adjust text size, color and position in Style, then set typing speed, punctuation pauses and a blinking cursor in Motion.

  3. Preview and export

    Press play and scrub the timeline to inspect individual frames. Choose your aspect ratio and background in Canvas, then download the format that fits your project. Pick one of six keyboard sound packs to include synchronized typing sounds, or mute sounds for a silent export.

Typing animation generator features

Natural typing timing

Set the interval between characters, add timing variation and give punctuation room to breathe. Start delay and end hold control how the animation opens and finishes.

Fonts, cursors and backgrounds

Try a monospace terminal, a serif quote or a bold title. Pick a bar, block or underline cursor with its own color and blink speed. Use a solid, gradient or transparent background.

Sizes for your next project

Use 16:9 for video intros and presentations, 9:16 for vertical stories, 1:1 for square posts or 4:3 for a classic frame. Choose 720p or 1080p and 24, 30 or 60 FPS for video.

Choose the right export format

This typing animation generator exports video for editing and sharing, HTML for a standalone animation, or PNG when you need a single still frame.

Typing animation generator FAQ

What is a typing animation generator?

A typing animation generator turns your text into an animation that reveals one character at a time, like someone typing on a keyboard or typewriter. You can add a blinking cursor, natural timing and pauses at punctuation.

Can I export a typing animation with a transparent background?

Yes. Select Transparent in the Canvas tab, then export WebM, HTML or PNG. MP4 does not support transparency. Check WebM alpha compatibility with your video editor before using an overlay.

Can I make a looping typewriter effect?

Yes. Enable Loop for preview and standalone HTML playback. MP4 and WebM exports contain one animation cycle; repeat the clip in your video editor if you need a longer loop.

Does Typing Animation export GIF animations?

There is no GIF export. Choose MP4 or WebM for video, HTML for an animated web page, or PNG for a still frame.

Are keyboard sounds included in the download?

Yes. MP4, WebM and HTML exports include your selected keyboard sound pack when sound is enabled in the editor. Mute sounds for a silent export. HTML playback with sound starts when you click Play. PNG is a still image and has no audio.

Do I need an account, and where is my project saved?

Editing and previewing do not require an account. Sign in to download animations or project files. Your project is automatically saved in this browser. Download a JSON project file with Save project to keep a backup or move to another browser. Clearing site data removes the browser's saved project.
