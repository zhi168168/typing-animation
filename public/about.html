About Typing Animation

Typing Animation is a browser-based typing animation generator at typing-animation.com. It helps you turn words into typewriter animations for videos, presentations, websites and social posts.

From a sentence to an animation

Start with your text, then choose fonts, backgrounds, cursor styles and typing speed. Adjust pauses and preview the result in the editor. Sign in to download MP4 video, transparent WebM video, a standalone HTML animation or a PNG image.

Your project stays in your browser

The hosted editor saves your current project on this device and generates exports in your browser. Signing in does not back up or sync your animation projects. Download a project file if you want to keep a backup or move to another browser.
