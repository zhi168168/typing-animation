2. Information we process

  • Editor content: your project, animation text and preferences are stored in your browser. On the hosted website, animation files are generated on your device; signing in does not upload them to the account database or create a cloud backup. Content you voluntarily send to support is processed to resolve your request.

  • Payments, when enabled: account-linked buyer identity, order and product IDs, amounts, currency, payment/refund status, subscription dates and download-entitlement/consumption records. Waffo Pancake receives the information needed for checkout, including a buyer identifier and account email. Full card details and CVCs are handled by its hosted checkout and payment partners; we do not store them.
  • Support: your email, message contents and relevant transaction or troubleshooting information.
  • Technical information: hosting and security systems may process IP addresses, browser/device information, request times, errors and operational logs. If you opt into analytics, Google Analytics processes page visits and usage/device information.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process account and billing data to perform our contract with you, including sign-in, downloads, subscriptions and customer support. We use necessary technical data for legitimate interests in security, abuse prevention and service reliability, balanced against your rights. We retain records when required by legal obligations. Optional analytics is based on your consent; declining it does not prevent editing or account access. We send necessary account, billing and security messages, not a marketing newsletter.

4. Cookies, browser storage and analytics choices

Essential session cookies maintain sign-in. Local storage saves projects, editor preferences and your privacy choice; session storage may preserve your pending export across sign-in or checkout. You can clear these through browser settings, but doing so may sign you out or remove local projects. Download a project backup first.

Google Analytics loads only after you choose Allow analytics. You can select Essential only or change your decision with . Withdrawal stops future analytics collection by our tag and removes accessible first-party Analytics cookies. It does not erase information previously processed by Google. We strip URL queries and fragments from analytics page addresses/referrers and do not intentionally send animation text, email addresses, passwords or payment tokens. Advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled. Read Google's Privacy Policy and its partner-site explanation.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose only information needed to provide the service to providers including Vercel (hosting/APIs), Neon (account database), our transactional email provider (delivery of requested account messages), Google (optional sign-in and consented analytics), and Waffo Pancake (checkout, payments, subscriptions, fraud prevention and refunds). The hosted checkout links to its applicable privacy terms. Payment providers may retain information to meet their own legal obligations.

We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect rights and service security, with your direction/consent, or in a business transfer subject to continuing privacy protection and any required notice.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords and authenticated access to account functions. Limit access to your account and do not send passwords, CVCs or verification links to support. No system is completely secure. If a reportable incident occurs, we will notify authorities and affected users within applicable legal deadlines, including the GDPR's 72-hour authority-notification requirement where applicable.

7. Retention

  • Local projects: remain on your device until you clear or replace them. We cannot remotely recover or erase your browser storage.
  • Account profile and sessions: retained while your account exists; confirmed deletion removes the active profile and linked sessions. We aim to complete any remaining deletion review within 30 days, unless an applicable exception requires retention. Canceling a subscription alone does not delete an account.
  • Payment and accounting records: retained for up to 7 years after a transaction for accounting, fraud and dispute handling, or longer only where legally required. These include limited identifiers needed to associate records with a transaction. Download entitlements remain while valid.
  • Support correspondence: retained for up to 2 years after a request is closed, unless needed for an unresolved dispute or legal obligation.
  • Operational logs and backups: held under the hosting/database providers' applicable retention and backup schedules; isolated retained copies are not used to recreate deleted accounts. Contact us for the schedule applicable to your request. We do not promise immediate erasure from independent provider backups.
  • Analytics: your browser choice remains until you change or clear it. Google handles collected analytics under its product retention settings and policy; you may ask us about deletion of identifiable information we can reasonably locate.

At the end of the applicable period, we delete, anonymize or restrict records as appropriate. Where retention is necessary for a legal claim or obligation, we retain only the information needed for that purpose.
