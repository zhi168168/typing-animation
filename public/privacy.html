Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy describes how Typing Animation at typing-animation.com handles information when you create typing animations or use an optional account.

Your animations

The hosted editor saves your current project and preferences in this browser. HTML, PNG and video exports are generated on your device. Animation text and frames are not sent to the account database. Signing in does not create a cloud backup. Clearing browser data removes locally saved projects, so download a project file to keep a copy.

Account information

If you register, we process your name, email address, password hash, verification records and session information. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive basic identity information such as your account identifier, name, email address and profile image, along with authentication records needed for that sign-in. We do not request access to Gmail messages or Google Drive files.

How information is used

Account data is used to provide sign-in, verify your email, send requested password-reset and deletion messages, and protect the service from abuse. Authentication uses essential session cookies. Browser storage also remembers your editor preferences and whether you dismissed the getting-started guide.

Service providers

The hosted service uses Vercel for website and account API hosting, Neon PostgreSQL for account storage, a configured transactional email provider for account messages, and Google when you choose Google sign-in. These providers may process information in other countries under their own service terms. Hosting and security systems may process IP addresses, request metadata and operational logs. We do not sell account information.

Retention and your choices

Account records are kept while your account exists. You can sign out, change your profile name, or request account deletion from the account page. Deletion requires an active session and confirmation through email; it removes the active account and linked sessions. Operational logs or provider backups may remain for their applicable retention periods. Deleting your Typing Animation account does not delete your Google account or the animation saved on this device.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email zhihaosong168@gmail.com or visit our contact page. Do not send passwords, tokens or private sign-in links. Use the account page for self-service account and deletion requests.
